PostgreSQL is an advanced open-source relational database management system.

It supports transactions, indexing, foreign keys, and complex SQL queries.

The ACID properties are Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability.

Atomicity ensures that a transaction either completes entirely or does not happen at all.

Consistency ensures that database constraints remain valid before and after a transaction.

Isolation controls how concurrent transactions interact with each other.

Durability ensures that committed transactions survive system failures.
